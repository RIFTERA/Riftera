FAQ

  
免费领养真的不用钱吗？Is the free mint really free?
铸造费为 0，只需要一点点 BNB 作为 Gas（通常远低于 0.0001 BNB）。前 100 只，每个钱包 1 只。The mint costs 0; you only pay a tiny bit of BNB gas. First 100 beasts, 1 per wallet.

  
免费、斥候、猎人有什么区别？Free vs Scout vs Hunter?
萌兽完全一样，稀有度随机；区别只是价格和元数据里的「Tier」标签。每种方式每个钱包各 1 只，最多 3 只。The beasts are identical with random rarity; only the price and the Tier label differ. One of each per wallet, 3 max.

  
图片存在哪里？Where is the art stored?
直接在合约里。tokenURI 在链上生成 SVG 和元数据，没有 IPFS，没有服务器，永远不会丢。Inside the contract. tokenURI builds the SVG and metadata on-chain. No IPFS, no server, it never disappears.

  
手机上连不上钱包？Wallet won't connect on mobile?
普通 Chrome/Safari 没有钱包。请在 MetaMask、Trust Wallet、OKX 或币安钱包 App 的内置浏览器里打开本页。Plain Chrome or Safari has no wallet. Open this page inside the MetaMask, Trust, OKX or Binance Wallet app browser.

  
怎样避免诈骗？How do I stay safe?
只使用本页公布的合约地址。领养只调用 freeMint / mintScout / mintHunter，不需要任何代币授权或签名消息。永远不要泄露助记词。Only use the contract address on this page. Minting calls freeMint / mintScout / mintHunter only, with no token approvals or signed messages. Never share your seed phrase.
