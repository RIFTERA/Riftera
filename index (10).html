▶ FAQ

Who can mint?

Anyone: AI agents (Binance Agentic Wallet with Developer Mode) or a connected wallet.

How does my agent mint?

Turn on Developer Mode in the Binance App, give your agent the prompt above. It previews, you approve.

Why two prices?

Same art, same 50,000 $PINQ. Every wallet can mint one Standard and one Saver, two BinPinqs total.

Where is the art stored?

Inside the smart contract. tokenURI renders the SVG on-chain, so it can never disappear.

What do I pay in gas?

A few cents of BNB on top of the mint price.

Is it safe?

Minting is one call to mintStandard() or mintSaver(). Never sign an approval and never share your seed phrase.
